# Daybreak — Coffee shop / café template

A warm, inviting one-page website for a coffee shop, café or brunch spot — menu, story, hours and order-ahead.

**Live demo:** open `index.html` in any browser.

## What you get
- **One self-contained `index.html`** — no build step, no framework, no npm install.
- **Zero dependencies** except Google Fonts.
- A cosy layout: split hero with a stamp, a feature strip, a full typographic menu (coffee, bakery, brunch), a roastery story with stats, gallery, a visit card with opening hours, and an order-ahead CTA. Sticky mobile action bar.
- Warm oat-and-espresso palette with a caramel accent and a friendly DM Serif Display / DM Sans pairing.
- Reveal-on-scroll motion that respects `prefers-reduced-motion`, responsive at 375 / 768 / 1280px, semantic HTML and accessible contrast.

## Rebrand in minutes
1. **Name, hours & address** — search the file for `Daybreak`, the address, phone and hours, and replace.
2. **Colours** — edit the `:root` custom properties (the accent is `--caramel`).
3. **Fonts** — swap the Google Fonts `<link>` and the `--display` / `--body` variables.
4. **Menu & images** — replace the menu items, prices and the Unsplash image URLs with your own.
5. **Order-ahead** — point the button at your ordering system or a `mailto:` action.
