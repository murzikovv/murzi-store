Our coffee

Roasted up the road

We roast in small batches a few streets away, so the beans in your cup were green a week ago, not a year. We rotate single-origins through the season and keep a dependable house blend on bar.

Decaf is real coffee here, oat and almond never cost extra, and the baristas will happily geek out about extraction if you let them.
