The curriculum

Six weeks, start to portfolio piece.

Each week pairs a live session with a real assignment and a personal critique. You leave with a finished project, not just notes.

Week 01

Seeing clearly

How trust is built and broken in an interface, and how to audit your own work like a stranger would.

  • Heuristics
  • Visual audit
  • Critique 101
Week 02

Type & hierarchy

Building a type system that does the heavy lifting, so the page reads itself in the right order.

  • Scale
  • Rhythm
  • Pairing
Week 03

Color & contrast

Committing to a palette with intent, and keeping it accessible without making it boring.

  • OKLCH
  • Contrast
  • Theming
Week 04

Layout & space

Composition, grids and the negative space that makes everything else feel inevitable.

  • Grids
  • Density
  • Responsive
Week 05

Motion & feedback

Interaction and motion that confirms, guides and delights without ever showing off.

  • Easing
  • Micro-states
  • Reduced motion
Week 06

Ship & show

Polishing the final project and presenting it the way the best designers talk about their work.

  • Polish
  • Story
  • Portfolio

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need to be a designer already?

You should be comfortable in a design tool and have shipped a few things. This is about craft and judgement, not the basics of the software.

What if I miss a live session?

Every session is recorded and yours forever. You can do the assignment on your own schedule and still get a critique.

How much time per week?

Plan for about five hours: two live, three on the assignment. The people who get the most out of it spend a little more.
