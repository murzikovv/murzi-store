# Brightwork — Home services / local trade template

A bright, trust-first website for plumbers, electricians, handymen and remodeling crews — built to turn visitors into free-quote requests.

**Live demo:** open `index.html` in any browser.

## What you get
- **One self-contained `index.html`** — no build step, no framework, no npm install.
- **Zero dependencies** except Google Fonts.
- A conversion-focused layout: trust badges, services grid, a clear four-step process, stats, work gallery, reviews, service areas, FAQ and a quote form.
- Sticky "Call / Free quote" bar on mobile, sticky desktop nav, working FAQ accordions, and a quote form that confirms on submit.
- Reveal-on-scroll motion that respects `prefers-reduced-motion`, responsive at 375 / 768 / 1280px, semantic HTML and accessible contrast.

## Rebrand in minutes
1. **Name & phone** — search the file for `Brightwork` and `(555) 010-0199` and replace.
2. **Colours** — edit the `:root` custom properties at the top of `<style>` (the amber accent is `--amber`).
3. **Fonts** — swap the Google Fonts `<link>` and the `--display` / `--body` variables.
4. **Copy & images** — replace the placeholder text and the Unsplash image URLs with your own crew and jobs.
5. **Quote form** — point it at your form handler, Formspree, or a `mailto:` action.
