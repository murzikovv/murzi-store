The residence

Designed around the view, and the light.

The Marin sits on a quiet crest with the whole bay laid out below it. Inside, the architect kept things calm: oak floors, plaster walls, and glass wherever a wall would have blocked the horizon. It's a house that feels bigger than its footprint and quieter than its address.

One room that holds the whole day.

The kitchen, dining and living spaces flow into one another and out onto the terrace, so morning coffee and a dinner for twelve happen in the same calm room.
