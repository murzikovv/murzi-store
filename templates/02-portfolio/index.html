I spent a decade inside agencies and one venture-backed product team before going independent. That mix means I think in systems and ship in pixels: a name, a logo, a type scale, and the live screens that use them, without three vendors and a lost month between them.

I work in focused engagements, usually four to eight weeks, with one founder in the room. Fewer clients, more care.
