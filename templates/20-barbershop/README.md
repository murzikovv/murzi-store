# Hollow & Crane — Barbershop template

A charcoal-and-brass barbershop site: price list, barbers, gallery, hours and booking. Masculine, classic, no-nonsense.

**Live demo:** open `index.html` in any browser.

## What you get
- **One self-contained `index.html`** — no build step, no framework, no npm install.
- **Zero dependencies** except Google Fonts.
- A booking-focused layout: cinematic hero, a full two-column price list, barber profiles, shop gallery, a review quote, find-us / hours cards and a booking CTA. Sticky mobile call/book bar.
- Tall condensed display caps (Oswald) on charcoal with a worn-brass accent; black-and-white photography that colors on hover.
- Reveal-on-scroll motion that respects `prefers-reduced-motion`, responsive at 375 / 768 / 1280px, semantic HTML and accessible contrast.

## Rebrand in minutes
1. **Name & contact** — search the file for `Hollow & Crane`, `(555) 010-0131` and the address, and replace.
2. **Colours** — edit the `:root` custom properties (the accent is `--brass`).
3. **Fonts** — swap the Google Fonts `<link>` and the `--disp` / `--body` variables.
4. **Prices, crew & images** — replace the price rows, barber cards and Unsplash image URLs with your own.
5. **Booking** — point the buttons at your booking system (Booksy, Fresha…) or a `mailto:` action.
