Our story

How we got here

We met on a rainy Tuesday in a bookshop café, both reaching for the last copy of the same novel. Theo let Aria have it — on the condition she'd tell him how it ended over coffee.

Seven years, three apartments and one very spoiled dog later, that coffee turned into a lifetime. We can't wait to celebrate the next chapter with the people we love most.

— Aria & Theo
