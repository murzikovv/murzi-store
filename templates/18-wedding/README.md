# Forever — Wedding / event invitation template

An elegant, romantic one-page wedding website — share the day, tell your story and collect RSVPs.

**Live demo:** open `index.html` in any browser.

## What you get
- **One self-contained `index.html`** — no build step, no framework, no npm install.
- **Zero dependencies** except Google Fonts.
- Everything a wedding site needs: a hero with a **live countdown**, your story, schedule of events, venue & directions, gallery, FAQ, gift registry and an **RSVP form**. Sticky mobile RSVP bar.
- A calm, editorial aesthetic: a flourishing Tangerine script over Cormorant Garamond and Inter, on warm ivory with sage, forest and a whisper of gold.
- Reveal-on-scroll motion that respects `prefers-reduced-motion`, responsive at 375 / 768 / 1280px, semantic HTML and accessible contrast.

## Rebrand in minutes
1. **Names & date** — search the file for `Aria`, `Theo`, the date and venue, and replace. Update the countdown target date in the script at the bottom.
2. **Colours** — edit the `:root` custom properties (accents are `--sage`, `--forest`, `--gold`).
3. **Fonts** — swap the Google Fonts `<link>` and the `--script` / `--display` / `--body` variables.
4. **Copy & images** — replace the story, schedule, FAQ and the Unsplash image URLs with your own photos.
5. **RSVP & registry** — point the form at your form handler (Formspree, Google Forms) and the registry links at your real registries.
