# Halo — Hair &amp; beauty salon template

A warm, boutique website for a hair, nail or beauty salon — built to fill the appointment book.

**Live demo:** open `index.html` in any browser.

## What you get
- **One self-contained `index.html`** — no build step, no framework, no npm install.
- **Zero dependencies** except Google Fonts.
- A booking-focused layout: hero, a full price service menu, gallery, stylist team, a 5-star quote, and a booking form with opening hours. Sticky mobile "Book a chair" bar.
- A soft, friendly aesthetic: Fraunces display serif on warm cream with a terracotta-rose accent.
- Reveal-on-scroll motion that respects `prefers-reduced-motion`, responsive at 375 / 768 / 1280px, semantic HTML and accessible contrast.

## Rebrand in minutes
1. **Name & contact** — search the file for `Halo`, `(555) 010-0177` and the address, and replace.
2. **Colours** — edit the `:root` custom properties (the accent is `--rose`).
3. **Fonts** — swap the Google Fonts `<link>` and the `--display` / `--body` variables.
4. **Menu, copy & images** — replace the service prices, text and the Unsplash image URLs with your own.
5. **Booking form** — point it at your booking system, Formspree or a `mailto:` action.
