Questions

Good to know.

Is the consultation really private and obligation-free?

Yes. The first consultation is a private conversation with your surgeon. You leave with options and a written quote, and there is never any pressure to book.

Are your surgeons board-certified?

Every surgeon at Aurelia is fully board-certified, and all procedures take place in our accredited surgical facility with specialist anaesthesia.

What does recovery look like?

It depends on the procedure, and we'll walk you through it in detail beforehand. You'll have scheduled reviews and direct access to your care team throughout.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We offer several payment plans, which we'll outline alongside your written quote so you can decide with the full picture.
