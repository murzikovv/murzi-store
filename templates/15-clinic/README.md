# Aurelia — Aesthetic / plastic surgery clinic template

A calm, couture website for an aesthetic and plastic surgery clinic — built to turn quiet, high-intent visitors into booked consultations. Rebrands cleanly to a **dental / aesthetic-dentistry** clinic.

**Live demo:** open `index.html` in any browser.

## What you get
- **One self-contained `index.html`** — no build step, no framework, no npm install.
- **Zero dependencies** except Google Fonts.
- A trust-building layout: hero, accreditation strip, treatments grid, "our approach", surgeon profiles, four-step process, stats band, testimonials, FAQ and a private booking form with a sticky mobile "Book" bar.
- An elegant high-contrast serif (Cormorant Garamond) paired with Inter, a warm ivory palette and a quiet bronze accent.
- Reveal-on-scroll motion that respects `prefers-reduced-motion`, responsive at 375 / 768 / 1280px, semantic HTML and accessible contrast.

## Rebrand in minutes
1. **Name & contact** — search the file for `Aurelia`, `+1 (555) 010-0100` and the address, and replace.
2. **Colours** — edit the `:root` custom properties (the accent is `--bronze`).
3. **Fonts** — swap the Google Fonts `<link>` and the `--display` / `--body` variables.
4. **Copy & images** — replace the placeholder text and the Unsplash image URLs with your own clinic, surgeons and treatments.
5. **Booking form** — point it at your form handler, scheduler, or a `mailto:` action.

### Turning it into a dental clinic
Swap "surgery / surgeons" wording for "dentistry / dentists", change the treatment cards (implants, veneers, whitening, orthodontics, hygiene), and update the imagery. The structure and trust elements carry over unchanged.
