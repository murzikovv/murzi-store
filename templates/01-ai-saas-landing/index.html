What Beacon does

Most monitoring tells you something broke. Beacon tells you what to do about it.

Three jobs, handled end to end: notice the drift, find the cause, hand you the fix.

/ detection

Catches drift before thresholds do

Static alerts fire when a number crosses a line you guessed months ago. Beacon learns each signal's normal rhythm, including its daily and weekly shape, and flags the moment behaviour breaks pattern.

  • Per-signal seasonal baselines, no manual thresholds
  • Sub-second detection on streaming metrics
  • Severity scored by blast radius, not raw size
/ correlation

Connects the spike to the change

Every anomaly is cross-referenced against deploys, feature flags, config edits and infra events on the same timeline. You get the likely cause ranked by correlation, not a wall of dashboards to read yourself.

  • Deploy, flag and config events on one timeline
  • Ranked root-cause candidates with evidence
  • One-click rollback of the implicated change
/ delivery

Reaches the one person who can fix it

Beacon knows who owns the implicated service and routes the alert straight to them, with the trace, the diff and the suggested action attached. No triage channel, no 2am page for the wrong team.

  • Ownership routing from your service catalog
  • Full context in Slack, PagerDuty or email
  • Auto-resolves and learns when the signal recovers
