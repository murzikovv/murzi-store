# Ledger — Budget & Cashflow Tracker

A small-business budget and cashflow tracker in Excel (`.xlsx`). Type your
income and expenses; the dashboard, monthly net, and charts update themselves.

## What's inside
`ledger-tracker.xlsx` — three linked sheets:

- **Dashboard** — four live KPIs (income, expenses, net profit, margin), a
  month-by-month table, and two auto-updating charts (income vs expenses, net trend).
- **Income** — one row per source, 12 months across, totals on the right.
- **Expenses** — one row per cost, 12 months across, totals on the right.

## How to use
1. Open `ledger-tracker.xlsx` in Excel, Numbers, or Google Sheets.
2. Go to the **Income** and **Expenses** sheets and type your figures.
3. The Dashboard recalculates instantly — nothing else to wire up.

All formulas are standard `SUM`; no macros, no add-ins, safe to share.
